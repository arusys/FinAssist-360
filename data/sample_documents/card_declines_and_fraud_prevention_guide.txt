# DEBIT & CREDIT CARD TRANSACTION DECLINES AND DISPUTE POLICY
Document ID: POL-CARD-2024-03
Department: Cards & Fraud Risk Management
Category: Debit and credit card support
Last Updated: September 2024

1. COMMON DECLINE RESPONSE CODES
- Code 05 - Do Not Honor: Generic decline issued by the card issuer's risk engine. Typically caused by geographic anomaly, unusual ticket size, or high velocity transactions.
- Code 51 - Insufficient Funds: Account available balance is less than transaction authorization request amount.
- Code 54 - Expired Card: The expiry MM/YY provided during authorization does not match the card record.
- Code 57 - Transaction Not Permitted: International usage or online e-commerce transactions disabled via customer mobile app controls.
- Code 61 - Exceeds Withdrawal Limit: Customer has exceeded daily POS/ATM limit configured on the card tier.

2. FRAUD DETECTION & COMPROMISED CARD PROTOCOL
When suspicious transactions are identified:
1. Immediately place a temporary authorization lock (Code 78 - Blocked Card) on the affected card.
2. Send automated multi-channel alert (SMS & Push Notification) to registered mobile number asking the customer to confirm or deny the transaction.
3. If confirmed unauthorized:
   - Permanently cancel the card number.
   - Issue a replacement virtual card instantly within the mobile application.
   - Ship physical contactless chip card within 3 business days.
   - Initiate chargeback claim with Visa/Mastercard/RuPay settlement networks.

3. CHARGEBACK FILING DEADLINES
Cardholders must submit formal dispute declarations within 60 calendar days from the statement transaction date. Temporary credit will be provided within 7 business days pending merchant representment.
